# ComeVisit v6 — Attention Now

## Working thesis

ComeVisit is not a directory of places and not an events calendar. It is a live local attention market for things that matter now.

Consumer job:
> What deserves my attention today?

Supply job:
> I have something relevant today / tonight / tomorrow / this weekend. I want people to see it.

Monetization:
> Publishing is free. Organic discovery is free. Extra attention is bought transparently in public.

Long term:
> Live activity creates history. History creates boards, status and crowns.

## The object

The primary object is a temporary local post, not a permanent place.

Minimum fields:
- title
- short description
- city
- neighborhood / area
- category
- starts_at
- expires_at
- price (optional)
- organizer / place name
- external CTA URL or WhatsApp
- status

Optional later:
- capacity / remaining spots
- recurring schedules
- ticketing
- reservations
- inventory sync

## MVP scope

Time tabs:
- AHORA
- HOY
- ESTA NOCHE
- MAÑANA
- FINDE

Categories remain broad and human:
- Comer
- Tomar algo
- Música
- Cultura
- Experiencias
- Bienestar
- Aire libre
- Aprender

Posts can be created by organizers, venues, locals or community contributors.

No prelisting requirement. No need to scrape the whole city. Empty states are valid.

## Ranking model

Two clearly separated layers:

### Sponsored attention
- explicit PATROCINADO label
- public bid / spend
- transparent take-the-lead price
- ranked by current market spend
- temporary market windows

### Organic discovery
- never influenced by money
- can sort by recency, starts soon, proximity, saves, curator signal or editorial/community logic
- no fabricated quality score

## Why short-term matters

Short-term relevance solves both sides:
- publisher has urgency to distribute now
- consumer has reason to come back because the feed changes every day

The product should feel alive because posts expire.

## Boards and prestige

Do not lead with static rankings in MVP.

Boards come after usage exists:
- Most attention this week
- Most #1 finishes
- Most saved this month
- Palermo this week
- Past winners
- Most active organizer

Principle:
> LIVE first. ARCHIVE after.

## Positioning

Primary consumer message:
> ¿Qué merece tu atención hoy?

Primary supply message:
> ¿Tenés algo que merece atención ahora? Publicalo.

Core rule:
> Pagar compra atención. No compra prestigio.

Business doctrine:
> Fill the room.

## MVP non-goals

Do not build yet:
- native ticketing
- payouts
- refunds
- seat-level inventory
- complex recurring events
- heavy integrations
- full city scraping
- broad review system
- permanent star ratings

## Validation

We need to learn whether:
1. people come back for a changing short-term local feed,
2. organizers will self-publish without us sourcing everything,
3. some organizers will pay for more attention,
4. public sponsored ranking creates understandable value without damaging trust.
